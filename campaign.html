About The Campaign

We're creating something extraordinary. This independent project represents months of passion, creativity, and dedication from a talented team. Your support directly enables us to bring this vision to life with the quality and care it deserves.

Every contribution, no matter the size, makes a meaningful difference. Together, we're proving that independent creators can produce world-class content without compromising on vision or integrity.

Why Your Support Matters

When you support this project, you're not just funding a product—you're investing in the creative vision of artists who've poured their hearts into this work. Every rupee goes directly toward bringing stories to life that wouldn't exist otherwise.

In a world where creativity is often dictated by algorithms and market trends, independent projects like this represent something rare: pure artistic expression. Your support gives creators the freedom to make bold choices, take creative risks, and deliver something genuinely original.

This isn't just about the final product. It's about proving that people still care about quality storytelling. It's about showing that there's an audience for work made with passion rather than profit margins. When you contribute, you become part of a movement that values creativity for its own sake.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will I receive my rewards?

Digital rewards are delivered after the official release of the project. You'll receive exclusive updates throughout the production process via Instagram Stories, and final rewards will be sent upon project launch.

Is my contribution secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard payment processing through Cashfree and support multiple payment methods. All transactions are encrypted and secure.

What if the project doesn't reach its goal?

We're committed to this project regardless. Even if we don't reach the full goal, we'll continue production and deliver the best possible result with the support we receive.

Can I contribute multiple times?

Absolutely! You can make multiple contributions. Each contribution is valued equally, and you'll receive rewards for each one.

How do I stay updated?

Contributors receive exclusive updates via Instagram Stories throughout the production process. You'll be the first to know about milestones, behind-the-scenes content, and project progress.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept online payments through Cashfree and manual UPI transfers. Both methods are secure and processed through our trusted payment partner.
