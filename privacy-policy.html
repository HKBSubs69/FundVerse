Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

Draft for legal review. This is a template prepared for FundVerse and has not yet been reviewed or approved by a qualified advocate. It should not be published or relied upon until reviewed by legal counsel.

This Privacy Policy explains how FundVerse, operated by Blue Ocean Studios India (an unregistered business/entity), collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website or make a contribution.

1. Information We Collect

When you make a contribution, we may collect:

  • Your full name and email address.
  • Your mobile number and country code, if provided (required for online payments through Cashfree; optional for Manual UPI contributions).
  • The contribution amount and the payment method you select.
  • For Manual UPI contributions, the UPI transaction ID you enter.
  • For Cashfree contributions, an order ID and payment status returned by Cashfree — we do not receive or store your card number, UPI PIN, CVV, or other sensitive payment credentials; these are handled directly and securely by Cashfree.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and verify your contribution.
  • To maintain accurate records of contributions for accounting and reconciliation.
  • To contact you regarding your contribution if necessary (e.g. to resolve a payment issue).
  • To display an aggregated, anonymized campaign total (total amount raised) publicly on the website — individual contributor details are never shown publicly.

3. Who Can Access Your Information

Individual contribution records — including your name, email, mobile number, and transaction details — are stored in a private database and are accessible only to authenticated administrators of FundVerse. They are never exposed on the public website. The public progress bar shows only an aggregate total, not individual records.

4. Third-Party Processors

Online payments are processed by Cashfree Payments (India) Pvt. Ltd. When you choose the Cashfree payment option, information necessary to process your payment (such as your name, email, and mobile number) is shared with Cashfree in accordance with their own privacy policy and applicable payment-industry security standards. We recommend reviewing Cashfree's privacy policy on their official website.

5. Data Storage & Security

Contribution data is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Access to this data is restricted through authentication and database security rules so that only signed-in administrators can view contribution records. Payment credentials used to authorize Cashfree transactions are kept in encrypted server-side secret storage and are never exposed to the browser or committed to any public code repository.

6. Data Retention

We retain contribution records for as long as reasonably necessary for accounting, reconciliation, and legal compliance purposes. If you wish to request deletion of your personal information, please contact us using the details on the FundVerse website, subject to any legal or accounting requirements that may require us to retain certain records.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by us. We will respond to reasonable requests within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Cookies & Analytics

This website may use minimal technical cookies or local storage required for its core functionality (such as reCAPTCHA and payment session handling). We do not currently use third-party advertising trackers.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to exercise your data rights, please reach out through the contact details provided on the FundVerse website.
