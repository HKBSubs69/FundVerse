Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

Draft for legal review. This is a template prepared for FundVerse and has not yet been reviewed or approved by a qualified advocate. It should not be published or relied upon until reviewed by legal counsel.

This policy explains how refunds and cancellations are handled for contributions made through FundVerse, operated by Blue Ocean Studios India.

1. General Policy

Contributions made through FundVerse are voluntary and support a specific creative project. Once a contribution/payment has been successfully completed, it is generally non-refundable, except where required under applicable law or explicitly stated otherwise in this policy.

2. Failed or Incomplete Payments

If a Cashfree payment fails, is cancelled by you before completion, or is not successfully captured, no amount is deducted or retained by us, and no contribution record is created. If an amount is deducted from your account but the payment is not confirmed as successful on our end, please contact us with your payment reference so we can investigate with Cashfree and arrange a refund of the deducted amount, where applicable.

3. Duplicate Payments

If you accidentally make a duplicate contribution for the same intended amount within a short period, please contact us with both transaction references. Duplicate charges that were not intentional will be reviewed and, where confirmed, refunded to the original payment method.

4. Manual UPI Contributions

For Manual UPI contributions, you are responsible for entering the correct transaction ID. We reserve the right to verify transaction details before treating a contribution as confirmed. If a Manual UPI contribution cannot be verified as received, we will contact you to resolve the discrepancy.

5. Change of Mind

Because contributions directly support ongoing project work, we generally do not offer refunds for a simple change of mind after a payment has successfully completed. If you have a genuine concern, please contact us and we will review it on a case-by-case basis.

6. How to Request a Refund Review

To request a review of a failed, duplicate, or disputed payment, please contact us through the details provided on the FundVerse website with your name, the amount, the date, and your transaction ID or Cashfree order ID. We aim to acknowledge refund-related queries within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Legal Rights

Nothing in this policy limits any statutory right you may have under applicable Indian consumer protection or payment regulations that cannot be excluded by agreement.
